Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 27 January 2027

KeyDhan (“KeyDhan”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you visit KeyDhan.com, submit an enquiry or request our services.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect your name, mobile number, email address, property preferences, enquiry details, service requests and information you choose to provide through forms, email, telephone or WhatsApp. We may also collect limited technical and usage information, such as browser, device and website interaction data, where applicable.

Please do not submit passwords, banking credentials or information unrelated to your enquiry through general website forms.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to property, loan, consultation and business-service enquiries.
  • To connect an interested person with a relevant seller, builder, developer or authorised representative.
  • To communicate about appointments, requested services, documents, fees and progress.
  • To operate, secure, maintain and improve the website and our services.
  • To maintain records, prevent misuse and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing Property Enquiries

When you submit a property enquiry, you understand that relevant details may be shared with the concerned seller, builder, developer or their authorised representative so they can respond to your request. We aim to share only information reasonably relevant to that enquiry.

4. Other Disclosures

Information may be shared with service providers who support website hosting, communications or business operations, and with public authorities or other persons where required or permitted by applicable law. This policy does not claim that personal information is never shared or sold independently of our actual practices.

5. WhatsApp and External Services

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp or another third-party platform, that platform may process information under its own terms and privacy policy. External websites linked from KeyDhan.com are not controlled by this policy; review their policies before sharing information.

6. Data Security and Retention

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to our operations to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, relevant recordkeeping, dispute resolution or legal requirements.

7. Your Privacy Requests

Subject to applicable law, you may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, or to raise a privacy concern. We may need to verify your identity and may retain certain records where the law requires or permits it.

8. Children

Our website and services are not intended to invite children to submit personal information. Where information relating to a child is involved, applicable legal requirements and safeguards must be followed.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may revise this Policy to reflect changes in our services, practices or legal requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact KeyDhan using the official contact details published on our website.

Note: This Policy describes intended handling of information and should be read alongside KeyDhan’s Terms & Conditions and Disclaimer. It must be applied consistently with our actual data practices and applicable law.
