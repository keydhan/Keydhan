Floating Home Loan Rates & EMI Reset

A floating-rate loan can change when its linked benchmark or applicable spread changes under the loan agreement. Depending on the lender's policy and the borrower's choice, a rate change may affect the EMI, the remaining repayment period, or both.

What to check in your sanction letter

  • The benchmark or reference rate to which the loan is linked.
  • The spread or margin and the circumstances in which it may change.
  • How often the interest rate is reset and when the next reset is due.
  • Whether a rate increase changes the EMI, tenure or a combination.
  • Applicable conversion, part-prepayment or foreclosure charges, if any.

What RBI guidance says

RBI's FAQs on reset of floating interest rates for EMI-based personal loans explain that regulated entities must communicate the impact of rate changes and provide specified options at reset, including adjustment of EMI and/or tenure, switching to a fixed rate subject to the lender's board-approved policy, and prepayment in part or full subject to applicable terms. The precise applicability and available options depend on the loan and regulated entity.

Questions to ask your lender

  1. What is my current benchmark, spread and effective rate?
  2. What changed, and from which date does the revised rate apply?
  3. Show me the revised EMI and/or remaining tenure in writing.
  4. What are the charges and conditions for switching or prepaying?
  5. Can I receive the updated amortisation schedule?

Official reading

  • RBI: FAQs on Reset of Floating Interest Rate on EMI-based Personal Loans
  • Reserve Bank of India – official website
Important: Do not assume that every floating-rate loan has identical reset terms. Read your agreement and request a loan-specific explanation from your lender.
