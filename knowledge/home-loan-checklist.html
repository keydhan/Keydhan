Home Loan Comparison Checklist

Comparing home loans only by the advertised interest rate can hide important differences. Review the complete repayment cost and the conditions that apply throughout the loan.

Compare these items in writing

  • Interest rate: Confirm whether it is fixed, floating or fixed for an initial period, and identify the benchmark and spread if applicable.
  • APR and total cost: Ask for the annual percentage rate or equivalent all-in cost disclosure and a full list of fees and charges.
  • Processing and other fees: Check processing, legal, technical, documentation, administrative and applicable third-party charges.
  • Reset terms: Understand the reset frequency, rate-change communication and how EMI or tenure may be adjusted.
  • Prepayment: Check part-payment and foreclosure conditions and any applicable charges under current rules and your loan type.
  • Disbursement: Confirm whether funds are released in stages, to whom they are paid and what documents trigger each tranche.
  • Repayment schedule: Request an amortisation schedule and understand how part-prepayments affect principal, EMI and tenure.
  • Property and borrower checks: Confirm legal/technical due diligence, required borrower documents, insurance conditions and any co-applicant obligations.

Use a like-for-like comparison

Compare the same loan amount and tenure across lenders. Include all compulsory charges and ask each lender to show the EMI, total interest, total fees and net disbursement. A lower initial EMI may result from a longer tenure and higher total interest.

Questions before signing

  1. Can I have the sanction letter, key fact statement and fee schedule before acceptance?
  2. Which charges are refundable if the loan is not sanctioned or disbursed?
  3. What happens if the benchmark changes?
  4. What are the conditions for cancellation, prepayment or foreclosure?
  5. Who will hold the original property documents and when will they be returned after repayment?
