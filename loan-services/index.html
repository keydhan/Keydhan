GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Does KeyDhan approve or sanction loans?

Loan approval, sanction, interest rates, fees and disbursement are decided solely by the relevant bank or financial institution under its policies and assessment.

What documents may be required?

Requirements vary by loan type, applicant profile and lender. Common documents may include identity and address proof, income or business documents, bank statements and property papers where relevant.

Can I check my estimated EMI?

Yes. Use the KeyDhan EMI Calculator for an indicative estimate. Actual EMI and loan terms depend on the lender's offer and final sanction.

Is submitting an enquiry a loan application?

No. An enquiry is only a request for a discussion. Any formal application, verification, credit assessment and approval process is handled according to the lender's requirements.

KeyDhan provides loan enquiry and facilitation assistance. KeyDhan is not a bank or NBFC and does not itself sanction or disburse loans. All financing is subject to lender eligibility criteria, verification, applicable charges and final approval. No approval, rate or disbursement timeline is guaranteed.
