Merge multiple PDF files into one document

Use KeyDhan Merge PDF to combine multiple PDF documents into a single file in the order you select. This is useful for joining reports, scanned documents, application pages or separate chapters.

How to merge PDF files

  1. Tap Choose files and select two or more PDF documents.
  2. Review the selected files and arrange their order as required.
  3. Tap Merge PDFs to combine the documents in your browser.
  4. Download and open the merged PDF to check page order and formatting.

Privacy and document compatibility

Your selected files are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to KeyDhan servers. Merging may not preserve every advanced feature in complex PDFs. Always review the output, especially if your documents contain forms, signatures, bookmarks or unusual embedded content.

Merge PDF FAQs

Can I combine more than two PDFs?

Yes. Select multiple PDF files, subject to the memory and processing limits of your device and browser.

Are the original files changed?

No. The tool creates a new merged file; your source documents remain on your device.

Can I reduce file size while merging?

Merging is not a compression guarantee. If you need to reduce size, try PDF Compressor after merging and review its image-based output limitations.
