PDF to Word: how to use this free online tool

Extract selectable PDF text into an editable Word document.

How to use the tool

Choose a text-based PDF, convert, download DOCX and review headings, spacing and paragraphs in a word processor.

Important notes and limitations

Scanned PDFs need OCR. Complex layouts and tables may not be preserved.

Frequently asked questions

Does this preserve the original document?

The tool creates an output copy. Keep your source document and review the generated file before relying on it.

Can I use this for official or legal purposes?

Check the recipient's technical and legal requirements. A converted document or visual annotation may not meet every formal submission or signature standard.
