KNOW YOUR CONNECTION

Understand Your Speed Test Results

A quick guide to what each number means and how to get a more reliable reading.

↔

Ping

Response time in milliseconds (ms). Lower ping generally means a more responsive connection.

↓

Download

How quickly data reaches your device. Higher download speed helps with streaming and downloads.

↑

Upload

How quickly your device sends data. Important for video calls, backups and sharing files.

⌁

Jitter

Variation in response time. Lower jitter generally supports smoother calls and real-time apps.

GET A BETTER READING

How to get a more accurate speed test

  • Pause large downloads, updates and video streams.
  • Move closer to your Wi-Fi router or test using a wired connection.
  • Close apps using the internet in the background.
  • Run two or three tests at different times and compare the results.
QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this show my ISP or public IP address?

No. This page does not display or save your public IP address or ISP details. The speed test request is handled by Cloudflare's test endpoint.

Is this an official TRAI speed test?

No. This is an independent browser-based test for general guidance. It is not a TRAI-certified measurement.

Why can my results vary?

Wi-Fi signal, network congestion, device performance, VPNs, background downloads, server routing and browser limitations can affect the result.

Will the test use my mobile data?

Yes. The test transfers data to measure download and upload speed. The amount can vary depending on your connection and test run.
