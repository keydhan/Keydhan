BMI Calculator: how to use this free online tool

Calculate adult Body Mass Index from height and weight as a general screening measure.

How to use the tool

Enter height in centimetres and weight in kilograms, calculate BMI and interpret as broad screening guidance only.

Important notes and limitations

BMI is not a diagnosis and does not directly measure body composition. Adult categories are not for children.

Frequently asked questions

Is this result official or a diagnosis?

No. This tool provides a calculation or general reference only. It does not replace official rules, professional assessment or advice where those are needed.

How can I improve result accuracy?

Enter accurate values, confirm units and dates, and review the displayed result before relying on it.
