Word Counter: how to use this free online tool

Count words and characters in text before preparing documents, applications or posts.

How to use the tool

Paste or type your text in the editor. Review the word and character totals, then revise the text and check the updated count. Counts can vary by app when hyphenated terms, symbols or languages are handled differently.

Important notes and limitations

Do not paste confidential information into a shared or public device. This tool is intended for general text counting.

Frequently asked questions

Are my files or inputs uploaded to KeyDhan?

Where browser processing is indicated, the selected content is handled on your device. Some tools need an internet connection or external reference data; the page explains those dependencies. Avoid using a shared device for confidential information.

Can I use the result for official submissions?

Check the receiving website's current format, dimensions, quality and legal requirements. Always review the exported result before submitting or sharing it.
