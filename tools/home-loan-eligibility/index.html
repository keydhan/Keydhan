How this home loan eligibility estimate works

The calculator estimates a monthly EMI capacity by applying the selected FOIR percentage to monthly net income and subtracting existing EMIs. It converts that EMI capacity into an approximate principal amount using the reducing-balance EMI formula.

Assumptions and limitations

FOIR is an editable scenario assumption, not a universal lender rule. Actual eligibility can differ due to income type, co-applicant income, age, credit history, obligations, property valuation, loan-to-value limits, fees and lender policy. The estimate excludes taxes, insurance and processing charges.

Frequently asked questions

Does this guarantee home loan approval?

No. Only a lender can assess an application and issue an approval or sanction.

Can I change FOIR?

Yes. Change the FOIR assumption to compare scenarios; confirm the applicable method with your lender.
