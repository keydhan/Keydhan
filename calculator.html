How to calculate your loan EMI

Enter the loan amount, annual reducing interest rate and repayment tenure. Choose months or years, then calculate your estimated monthly instalment. EMI depends on principal, interest rate and tenure; actual lender terms, fees and eligibility may differ.

What affects your EMI?

  • Loan amount: A higher principal generally increases EMI.
  • Interest rate: A higher rate generally increases total interest and EMI.
  • Tenure: A longer tenure can reduce monthly EMI but may increase total interest.

Frequently asked questions

Is the EMI result a loan offer?

No. It is an estimate for planning and does not represent approval, sanction or a lender quote.

Can I calculate home and business loan EMI?

Yes. Use the calculator with the applicable loan amount, rate and tenure for a planning estimate.
